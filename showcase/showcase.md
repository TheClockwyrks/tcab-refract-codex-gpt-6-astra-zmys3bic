# Refract

A quiet puzzle about making connections. On a dark optical bench, mint triangles, amber squares, and violet diamonds wait for you to draw them together. Press an outlined emitter and trace a beam of light from node to node.

Each channel has two endpoints. Join them through every matching lens, taking care not to cross another segment. Neutral crystals let channels meet, but each holds a precise charge: every crossing spends one, and every charge must be used.

Work through 24 crafted studies in Campaign, from a single luminous thread to dense arrangements of three intertwined channels. Every solved study opens the next, and you can return to earlier boards to find another way through.

Choose Cascade for an endless sequence of fresh puzzles across five rising tiers. There is no clock and no move limit. Release to keep your work, retrace a step to undo it, and take the time to see the whole picture.
